Add a domain

The name clients come with, and the site's server the node forwards to — http://203.0.113.7:8080, or just 127.0.0.1:3000 for http. Loopback and private addresses are fine: the site often lives on the same machine. A pattern like *.example.ru covers every subdomain.

Upload a certificate

A ready-made pair: the chain (fullchain.pem) and the private key (privkey.pem). The domains are taken from the certificate's own names. The pair is checked before it is accepted — the key must match, the term must not be over, at least one name must be a served domain — and starts serving at once. The node does not issue or renew certificates: renewal is uploading a fresh pair, or certbot next to the node.
