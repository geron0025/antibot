The name clients come with, and the site's server the node forwards to — http://203.0.113.7:8080, or just 127.0.0.1:3000 for http. Loopback and private addresses are fine: the site often lives on the same machine. A pattern like *.example.ru covers every subdomain. The certificate is uploaded in the domain's card once it is added.
